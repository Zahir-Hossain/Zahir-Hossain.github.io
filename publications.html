Peer-reviewed conference work from my undergraduate research.

Completed as part of my work with LaSSET at BRAC University, applying multispectral satellite imagery and machine learning to agricultural analysis.

2020, 11th International Conference on Electrical and Computer Engineering (ICECE)

"Agricultural Analysis and Crop Yield Prediction of Habiganj using Multispectral Bands of Satellite Imagery with Machine Learning"

Fariha Shahrin, Labiba Zahin, Ramisa Rahman, A. S. M. Jahir Hossain, Abdulla Hil Kafi, and A. K. M. Abdul Malek Azad

DOI ↗Thesis ↗

Habiganj's cropland was profiled from Sentinel multispectral bands, deriving vegetation indices used to relate spectral reflectance to crop condition. Regression and machine-learning models were then compared for predicting yield, evaluating which approach generalised best from the derived features.

  • Multispectral imagery
  • Crop yield prediction
  • Vegetation indices
  • Regression
  • Machine learning
