01 / Research

Selected research projects

01

Agricultural analysis and crop-yield prediction of Habiganj

Contributed to a study combining Landsat-8 multispectral imagery, remote-sensing indices, NASA Giovanni precipitation data and Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics crop-production data. The work compared K-means and Mask R-CNN for vegetation mapping; linear and Random Forest regression for yield prediction; and ARIMA and LSTM for forecasting.

Remote sensingMask R-CNNRandom ForestARIMALSTM
02

Multitemporal crop-type segmentation

Ongoing research project using Python and PyTorch to study crop segmentation from imagery acquired across time. The workflow emphasises reproducible experiments, evaluation on held-out data and performance reporting across classes.
